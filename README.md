# 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Regional Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance across different regions during **2025**.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business intelligence by providing insights into **regional revenue, sales trends, product/category performance, customer activity, and key business KPIs**.

It is designed as a professional analytical reporting solution for management and business stakeholders.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze regional sales performance
- Track revenue and sales KPIs
- Compare performance across regions
- Identify high-performing areas
- Analyze sales trends throughout 2025
- Evaluate product/category performance
- Support data-driven business decisions
